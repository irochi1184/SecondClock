## 最終プロンプト

> Use case: logo-brand.
> Asset type: iOS App Store app icon color refinement, candidate L “Emerald Tangerine”.
> Input images: Image 1 is the edit target. Preserve its exact two-line digital time concept and overall typography.
> Primary request: Give the clock a fresh, modern utility-app identity. Keep the top line exactly “12:34” and the bottom line exactly “:56”. Use a deep petrol-teal to saturated emerald full-bleed gradient with a soft aurora sweep. Upper time is clean soft white; seconds are vivid warm tangerine-orange for immediate recognition. Use restrained depth and a subtle halo behind the complete time mark.
> Composition/framing: retain the centered two-line layout, proportions, safe margins, and strong small-size silhouette.
> Style/medium: premium vector-friendly iOS icon, clear branded palette, visually rich but minimal, not a screenshot.
> Constraints: change palette, lighting, and finish only; render text exactly once; no outer rounded-square border; no panels; no extra symbols; no transparency; no watermark.
> Avoid: Christmas red-green pairing, dull gray teal, excessive texture, glow bloom, tiny details.
