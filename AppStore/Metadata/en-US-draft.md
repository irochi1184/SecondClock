Short description draft:

SecondClock is a clear, customizable full-screen clock that shows hours, minutes and seconds. Keep the display awake for a desk or bedside clock, use the OLED night mode, save reusable presets, switch them with a swipe or widget buttons, and add Home Screen or Lock Screen widgets. Pro removes the preset limit and adds animated backgrounds, flip and seconds-ring faces, scheduled day/night presets, timed night mode, photos, and a fixed preset for each widget. Widgets display matching static animated-background designs.
